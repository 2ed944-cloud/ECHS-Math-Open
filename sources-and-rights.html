Sources and publication boundaries

Important: an AP-aligned textbook question is not an official AP question. Practice Studio and the Official AP Centre use different source rules, labels, data stores and publication gates.

ECHS-created material

Original lessons, explanations, diagrams, practice questions and assessments created for the ECHS learning environment.

Publisher collections

Textbook or test-bank questions retained with publisher, edition, bank, chapter, pool and source metadata. Student labels do not remove canonical provenance.

Official AP archive

Separately indexed records governed by source, transcription, mathematical, answer, media, KaTeX and curriculum-mapping verification gates.

Attribution and provenance

Canonical records should retain available information about the source family, publication, edition, year, page or question number, source asset, import batch and audit history. A visual redesign must never erase provenance.

Official AP publication gate

Official archive records may enter student practice only after the repository’s independent release conditions are satisfied. Indexed, incomplete or review-required records remain restricted or redacted and are excluded from student scoring.

Publisher-bank publication gate

A publisher collection is described as complete only when the expected question count, chapter payloads, unique identifiers, answer references and required media packages pass the repository validation gate. Original duplicate variants may be retained when the source contains them.

Permissions

Availability within this school-operated repository does not automatically grant third parties permission to redistribute publisher or restricted assessment content. Users should follow the applicable licence, institutional permission and source terms.

Corrections

Source, transcription, answer, figure or attribution corrections should preserve the original record ID and audit trail. Verified content must not be silently overwritten.
