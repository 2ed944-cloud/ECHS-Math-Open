# Practice Studio student-label update

This update removes internal import terminology from student-facing pages.

- `Initial Import`, `Pilot`, and internal bank codes are not shown to students.
- `Blackboard Bank` is presented as `Textbook Collection`.
- `Pearson Precalculus Test Bank · Initial Import` is presented as `Pearson Precalculus · Functions and Relations`.
- Bank dropdowns show publisher titles rather than internal codes.
- The AP Precalculus snapshot counts unique question IDs and reports partial coverage honestly until the complete collections are committed.
- The Official AP Center remains a separate route and dataset.
