Accessibility

Platform goal: provide an inclusive mathematics experience that can be navigated by keyboard, read with assistive technology, resized, printed and used with reduced motion.

Current foundation

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, labels and skip navigation on the principal platform pages.
  • Keyboard-accessible course selection, filters, lesson controls and practice answer choices.
  • Visible focus indicators and support for the browser’s reduced-motion preference.
  • Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
  • Light and dark appearance options with persistent user preference.
  • Text alternatives for platform logos and source figures where available.
  • Status and answer feedback regions that can be announced by assistive technology.

Mathematics

Mathematical notation is rendered with KaTeX where supported. Source figures should include meaningful alternative text, while complex graphs may also require a nearby textual description, table or equivalent interpretation.

Known limitations

Some historical source-bank figures and older lesson files may not yet meet the same accessibility standard as the platform shell. Some publisher exports contain mathematical information embedded in images. These items remain candidates for progressive remediation rather than being silently described as fully accessible.

Quality target

The platform’s design and testing target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA for the student-facing interface. Automated checks must be combined with keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, colour-contrast and mobile testing.

Reporting a barrier

An accessibility issue should identify the page, lesson or question, the action being attempted, the browser or assistive technology used and the observed problem. Reports can be submitted through the project’s GitHub issue tracker.
